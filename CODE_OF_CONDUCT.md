# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Discuss geometry, algorithms and evidence constructively, and describe uncertainty honestly.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate misinformation and publication of another person's private or medical information are not acceptable. Keep reports focused on reproducible technical behavior.

The maintainer may edit, remove or close abusive content and restrict participation when necessary. GitHub platform abuse can also be reported through GitHub's reporting tools. Do not publish sensitive details to make a conduct report.
