# Third-party software and data

The MIT license covers this repository's original code, documentation and synthetic illustration/fixtures. No third-party application binaries, patient scans or anatomical mesh datasets are bundled.

3D Slicer, Gmsh, Abaqus and referenced datasets retain their own licenses and terms. The open-data integration examples used independently obtained data; their licenses are not replaced by this repository's MIT license. Source links and distinctions are recorded in `references/sources-and-validation.md`.

In particular, VSD/BoneHub data cited by the demonstration use CC BY-NC-SA terms; the referenced Universiti Malaya MRI release uses CC0. Users must check the actual source/version before downloading or redistributing data.
